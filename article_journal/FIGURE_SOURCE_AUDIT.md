figure workflow. The audit covers every image already present in the repository:

## Blocking constraint recorded at audit time

At the moment of this audit the machine was running an **active PPO training
campaign**:

```
PID 66308  python -m marine_race_arena.learning.gen2.train_ppo_27d_speed
           --out artifacts_gen2/ppo_27d_speed_campaign_B_20260908_retry6
           --max-timesteps 500000 --seed 20260908
```

with three worker subprocesses (PIDs 27240, 53932, 110528) each driving a
dedicated `Holodeck` engine instance (PIDs 47716, 58828, 77496). The training
writes into the **main** worktree, `C:\Users\andrea.bedei3\Desktop\HoloDroneCompetition`.

**Consequence: no figure in this manuscript launched HoloOcean.** Every raster
image is either an already-committed capture or a plot computed from frozen
artifacts. All three figure scripts under `article_journal/scripts/` record
`holoocean_launched_by_this_script: false` in their provenance JSON.
