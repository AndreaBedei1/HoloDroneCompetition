The audit covers the 68 released runs of the frozen benchmark matrix ---

Matched counts course ordinals at which both a referee crossing and a

of their run, and missed counts referee crossings with no corresponding local
advancement. Advancements of the second false kind are counted in both matched
and false, so local $=$ matched $+$ false $-$ 6 rather than matched $+$ false;
every one of them falls on Horseshoe Bay, whereas referee $=$ matched $+$ missed
