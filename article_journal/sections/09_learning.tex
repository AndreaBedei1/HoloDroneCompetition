\section{Learning-Based Extension}
\label{sec:learning}

Learning is included to test whether the benchmark contract is reusable beyond
hand-written controllers, not as a separate contribution. A learned policy
enters \mra{} through the same participant interface, official observation and
independent referee used by every other controller. The resulting evaluation
therefore tests both integration and the benchmark's ability to expose
long-horizon failure modes.

\subsection{Integration and Observation Encoding}
\label{sec:learning_obs}

A thin wrapper maps the official observation to the
\cmd{onboard\_local\_transition\_v1} encoding, calls the policy and returns the
four continuous body-frame actions of \eqref{eq:command}. Actions are clamped
and depth-limited by the same framework path as rule-based actions. At
evaluation time the policy receives no gate coordinates, global pose, referee
progress or reward signal.

The encoding has $35$ bounded features: acoustic beacon presence, bearing,
elevation, range, strength and age; visual target presence, centre, area and
confidence; depth and inertial/Doppler measurements with presence flags; the
previous four actions; and short-term visual/acoustic derivatives plus target
change timing. It is sequence-length independent: it describes the current
target and recent local dynamics, with no gate index, mission length or future
geometry. Missing measurements remain explicitly absent rather than imputed.
This makes a learned policy a local transition controller and leaves any
sequence-management capability to be measured by the benchmark.

\subsection{Action Space, Reward and Curriculum}
\label{sec:learning_reward}

The action space is the same four-channel vector in $[-1,1]$ used by the
reference controllers. PPO~\cite{schulman2017ppo}, implemented with
Stable-Baselines3~\cite{raffin2021sb3}, is trained with a bounded reliability-
oriented objective. Dense terms reward signed improvement in acoustic alignment,
visual centring and range, while crossing and completion provide sparse
milestones. Collisions, out-of-bounds motion, wrong-direction or missed gates,
stale acquisition and post-crossing orbiting are penalized. The reward is
constructed from bounded deltas of monotone quantities, so oscillation cannot
manufacture progress; the detailed audit and configuration remain in the
repository.

The curriculum addresses the central long-horizon question by varying sequence
length. Training progresses from single/local transitions to mixtures of short,
medium and progressively longer generated sequences, while retaining short
episodes as a source of target-switch experience. Stage promotion is based on
validation performance rather than elapsed training steps. The three official
circuits are excluded from training; all reported learning comparisons use
generated geometries and the registered official circuits only after the
checkpoint and evaluation protocol were frozen.

\subsection{Frozen Checkpoints and Evaluation Separation}
\label{sec:learning_readiness}

Checkpoint selection is separated from training. A frozen checkpoint is stored
with its model hash, source commit and training provenance, and the readiness
criteria are registered and hashed before evaluation. The final Generation-1
checkpoint was carried through the registered validation and holdout procedures
without manual override; the outcome is reported in Section~\ref{sec:eval_rq5}.
Full readiness records, training settings and seed registries are retained as
reproducibility artifacts rather than expanded here into a training log.

\subsection{Matched Evaluation Protocol}
\label{sec:learning_eval_protocol}

Learned, hybrid and rule-based controllers are evaluated with identical seeds,
generated geometries, official configurations, adapter settings and referee
logic. Manifests record the requested and actual adapter, fallback status,
current profile, timestep, contract versions and model or source hashes. A
policy-only result is kept separate from a hybrid result that blends learned
actions with a deterministic backbone. The official circuits were opened by an
earlier exploratory access ledger, so learning results on them are described as
retrospective diagnostic comparisons, not held-out generalization claims.
