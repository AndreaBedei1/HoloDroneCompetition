DATA AVAILABILITY STATEMENT
Marine Race Arena - Robotics and Autonomous Systems

The benchmark implementation, the official track configurations, the reference
controllers, the referee, the fleet layer, the learning pipeline and the
aggregated result artifacts that support the findings of this study are openly
available in the HoloDroneCompetition repository at
https://github.com/AndreaBedei1/HoloDroneCompetition

Specifically:

* Benchmark and controllers   marine_race_arena/
* Official track definitions  marine_race_arena/tracks/
* Learning pipeline           marine_race_arena/learning/
* Aggregated result artifacts results/rl_public/
* Perception audit captures   artifacts_gen2/visual_collection_smoke/
* Manuscript sources, tables,
  figures and figure scripts  article_journal/

Aggregated per-run summaries, event logs, experiment manifests, evaluation
manifests, model and track SHA-256 hashes, seed registries and readiness
verdicts are included in the repository. Every result table and figure in the
manuscript is regenerated from these artifacts by committed scripts; the
commands are listed in Section 13 (Reproducibility).

Two categories of file are intentionally not distributed through the
repository, and neither is required to verify any reported number:

1. Raw per-run artifact trees of the 78-run benchmark matrix, and neural-network
   weight files (*.zip, *.npz, *.npy) and rendered videos (*.mp4). These are
   large binary artifacts. The compact audit trail for each of them - the
   manifest, the aggregated metrics and the SHA-256 hash - IS included, so a
   reported value can be traced and checked without the binary.

2. Nothing else. No result reported in this paper depends on data that is
   withheld.

Raw artifacts and model weights are available from the corresponding author on
reasonable request.
